# Healthcare Analysis & Insights Dashboard 🏥📊

An end-to-end Excel Analytics & Interactive Dashboard project focused on exploring patient demographics, medical examination trends, surgical history, and healthcare cost patterns.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes patient health records to uncover critical patterns between patient demographics (Age, BMI, HbA1c, Smoking Status) and healthcare outcomes (Cancer History, Major Surgeries, Healthcare Charges). 

The output is an interactive **Excel Dashboard** equipped with multi-connected Slicers to dynamically filter metrics across all visualization panels.

---

## 📂 Dataset Architecture

The workbook consists of structured datasets detailing:
1. **Hospitalisation Details:** Patient registration, admission details, and tier classifications.
2. **Medical Examinations:** Physical metrics including BMI, HbA1c levels, blood pressure, and primary health indicators.
3. **Healthcare & Financials:** Billing details, treatment charges, and insurance coverage metrics.

---

## 🔑 Key Analyses & Visualizations

### 1. Cancer History Among Smokers vs. Non-Smokers 🚭
* **Type:** Donut Chart
* **Insight:** Evaluates the proportion of cancer diagnoses segmented by smoking status to illustrate health risks associated with smoking.

### 2. Major Surgeries & Average HbA1c by Transplant History 🩺
* **Type:** Pie Chart
* **Insight:** Displays the distribution of major surgical procedures and average blood sugar levels (HbA1c) grouped by organ transplant history.

### 3. Age-wise Trends in BMI and HbA1c 📈
* **Type:** Line Chart with Markers (Pivot Chart)
* **Insight:** Tracks average Body Mass Index (BMI) and HbA1c levels across age progression (18 to 65+ years) to identify age-related physiological changes.

### 4. Healthcare Charges by Age 💵
* **Type:** Line Chart with Markers (Pivot Chart)
* **Insight:** Illustrates how medical expenditures increase with age, highlighting peak cost demographics.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard & Features

* **Centralized Dashboard View:** Formatted with a sleek Dark Mode aesthetic for high-contrast presentation.
* **Global Slicers:**
  * `Weight Status` (Underweight, Normal Weight, Overweight, Obesity)
  * `Diabetes Status` (Normal, Prediabetes, Diabetes)
* **Report Connections:** All 4 Pivot Charts are dynamically linked to both Slicers, allowing real-time filtering across all visual components simultaneously.
